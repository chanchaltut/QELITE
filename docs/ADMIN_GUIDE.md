# Managing QELITE — quick guide

Sign in at **https://www.qelite.com/admin**, or use **Team login** at the bottom of any page.

## Certificates (live immediately)

**Add a certificate:** sidebar → *Add certificate*.
1. Type the **certificate number** exactly as printed, e.g. `QMS/26Q00001`. Each number can be used only once.
2. Choose the **standard** from the list, which fills in its name, or type it.
3. Enter the **organisation**, **address** and **scope** exactly as printed.
4. Enter the **date of initial registration**. The two surveillance dates and the re-certification date are filled in automatically (the day before each anniversary). You can change them.
5. Press **Add to register**. It can now be found on the website's Verify page.
6. **Upload the PDF** of the signed certificate (right-hand panel). Without one, visitors see the QELITE digital certificate instead.

**Print a QELITE certificate:** open the certificate → **Print / save as PDF**. In the print dialog, choose *Save as PDF*, paper *A4*, margins *None*, and tick *Background graphics*.

**Statuses**
| Status | What visitors see |
|---|---|
| Valid | "This certificate is valid", plus the certificate copy |
| Valid, past re-certification date | "Expired" (automatic) |
| Suspended | "Suspended", with no certificate copy |
| Withdrawn | "Withdrawn", with no certificate copy |

To cancel a certificate, set it to **Withdrawn** rather than deleting it. A deleted certificate shows as "not found", which looks the same as a fake one. Only the owner account can delete.

**The register list** has filters for Valid, *Audit due in 60 days*, Expired, Suspended and Withdrawn. **Export (CSV)** opens in Excel. The dashboard shows upcoming audits and which certificates visitors have checked recently.

## Website content (live after Publish)

1. **Save changes** on each page stores your edits. Visitors don't see them yet.
2. **Publish** (top right) puts everything saved on the website. It updates in about **1–2 minutes**, and the panel tells you when it's live.

| Menu | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Standards** | Every certification programme: page text, who it's for, benefits, audit focus, FAQs. Turn off "Show on website" to hide one, and "Feature on the home page" to choose the home-page cards. |
| **Certificate design** | Issuer name, certificate titles, signatory name and signature image, seals, accreditation code, small print, QR code on/off |
| **Accreditation & policies** | Accreditation details and the impartiality, complaints, appeals, suspension and mark-use policies |
| **Site settings** | Phone, WhatsApp, email, address, map, hours, social links, logo, announcement bar |
| **Home page** | Hero text, figures, why-us, certification journey, industries, testimonials, which sections show |
| **About, FAQs, Legal pages, Menu, Pages & SEO** | As named |
| **Inbox** | Quote requests, enquiries/complaints and reports of suspicious certificates, with status and notes |
| **Form settings** | Which email addresses get new-submission alerts |
| **Publish history** | Every publish. **Restore** puts the website back to an earlier version. |
| **Users** | Give colleagues their own login (Owner or Editor) |

## Good to know
- **Text in [square brackets]** means "not filled in yet" and is hidden on the website.
- **Red fields** need fixing before you can save.
- If two people edit the same item at once, the second save is stopped and nothing is overwritten. Reload and make your change again.
