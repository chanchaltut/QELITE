# Information needed from the client (QELITE)

Anything not yet provided is hidden on the live site until it is filled in. Once the site is live, all of it can be entered in the admin panel.

## 1. Brand & contact
- ✅ Final **logo** and favicon are in place across the site, certificates and portfolio PDF.
- Domain **qelite.in** and email **contact@qelite.in** are chosen, but the domain is not purchased yet
- **Legal name** of the certification body as it should appear on certificates (currently "QELITE Certification Services")
- **Office address**, Google Maps link, office hours
- **Phone** and **WhatsApp** numbers (the call/WhatsApp buttons appear once these are entered)
- Social media links, if any

## 2. Certificate design
- **Signatory**: name, designation and a signature image (transparent PNG)
- **Issuer address** lines to print on certificates
- **Accreditation code** and **seal/mark images**, but only marks QELITE is licensed to use
- Confirm the certificate titles: "Certificate of Registration" / "Certificate of Compliance"
- Confirm the **certificate numbering scheme** (e.g. `QMS/26Q00001`)
- Review the small print on the certificate

## 3. Accreditation & policies — important
- **Accreditation details**: the accreditation body, accreditation number, scope, validity, and a link where it can be checked. Until these are provided the Accreditation page says the details "will be published here". It makes no accreditation claim.
- **Review every policy text** (impartiality, confidentiality, complaints, appeals, suspension/withdrawal, use of marks). These are commitments QELITE must actually follow; edit any that don't match how QELITE operates.
- The "Why QELITE" points on the home page are commitments too (e.g. independent certification decisions, one fee for the full cycle). Please confirm or edit them.

## 4. Standards
- Confirm which of the 13 standards QELITE certifies. Hide any it doesn't, and tell us about any that are missing.
- Choose which standards to feature on the home page.

## 5. The real certificates (10–15)
- Enter them in the **production** admin panel only after go-live (*Add certificate*), and upload each signed PDF.
- No real certificate data is used in development. Local testing used fictitious specimen records only.
